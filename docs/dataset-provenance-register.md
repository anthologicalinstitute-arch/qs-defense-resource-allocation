Defense Resource Allocation — Dataset Provenance Register

Status: Draft v0.1

Purpose

Establish traceable public data sources for a defined, non-sensitive resource-allocation research problem.

Candidate sources to evaluate

- U.S. Department of Defense budget materials and public financial reports.
- U.S. federal budget datasets.
- NATO public defense expenditure reports.
- U.S. Government Accountability Office reports relevant to resource planning.

Required source record

For every selected dataset, record:

- Publisher and dataset title
- Official source URL
- Access date and publication date
- Reporting period and geographic coverage
- Units, currency, and definitions
- License or reuse conditions
- Missingness, revisions, and known limitations
- Local filename and integrity hash, when available

Comparability controls

Do not combine figures until fiscal years, currency, inflation treatment, accounting definitions, and reporting scopes have been reconciled.

Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each selected source is traceable to its publisher.
- [ ] Units and reporting periods are documented.
- [ ] Reuse conditions are reviewed.
- [ ] Known comparability limitations are recorded.
